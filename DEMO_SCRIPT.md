# 1-minute demo video script

Record at 720p (QuickTime/OBS/Loom). Keep it under 10 MB: on Mac, QuickTime → File → Export As → 720p, or compress at handbrake.fr (Fast 720p30, then lower quality to RF 28 if still big).

0:00–0:08  "This is a hiring dashboard for Arjun at Kargo. He has 60 CVs, two roles, and zero offers in eleven weeks."
0:08–0:18  Rubric page: "The rubric comes from his best past hires, not the job description. Both roles, weights add to 100."
0:18–0:30  Upload page: pick Product Manager, upload 2 CVs, show them scoring. "Name, email and phone are stripped on the server before anything goes to AI."
0:30–0:45  Dashboard: ranked list, the interview line, click the top candidate. Read the brief aloud. Show the criterion scores and reasons, and the score for the other role.
0:45–0:55  Show the draft invite with the real name. Click a candidate below the line to show the rejection draft. Back to the top one, click Confirm and send.
0:55–1:00  Show the inbox receiving it and the card marked Sent. "The system recommends. Arjun decides. That click is the last thing he touches."
